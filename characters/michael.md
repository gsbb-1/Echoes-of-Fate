# Michael

- **Role:** mysterious stranger, newcomer to Willow Creek
- **Wants:** searching for something he can't yet put into words
- **Obstacle:** a troubled past he keeps hidden behind a stoic facade
- **Backstory:** not yet established — needs the specifics of his past and
  why he came to Willow Creek specifically
- **Voice / persona:** wary, curious, guarded — not yet distinct in prose
  from the other characters in the Chapter 1 draft
- **Introduced:** Chapter 1, walking the town's streets, scanning faces
- **Arc:**
  - Ch. 1 — introduced, new in town, searching for something
  - Ch. 2 — meets Sarah; their meeting sets off the chain of events that
    intertwines their fates
  - Ch. 7 — relationship with Sarah deepens as they navigate their pasts
- **Relationships:** Sarah (love interest / catalyst)
- **Plot thread (resolved 2026-09-14):** Michael's older brother, Daniel,
  disappeared from Willow Creek eighteen years ago and was never found —
  Jack's unsolved case. Michael has come to town to find out what actually
  happened, working from what little he knows of Daniel's last days there.
