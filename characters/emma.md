# Emma

- **Role:** successful businesswoman, respected figure in Willow Creek
- **Wants:** to protect the carefully constructed life/reputation she's built
- **Obstacle:** a secret that threatens to unravel that life
- **Backstory:** not yet established — the secret itself is undefined
- **Voice / persona:** anxious under a composed exterior; not yet distinct
  in prose from the other characters in the Chapter 1 draft
- **Introduced:** Chapter 1, at her desk, glancing at the clock
- **Arc:**
  - Ch. 1 — introduced, anxious, hiding something
  - Ch. 3 — secret begins to surface
  - Ch. 6 — secret threatens to come to light, causing relationship tension
  - Ch. 8 — secret is revealed; must face the consequences
- **Relationships:** connected to Jack via his new case
- **Plot thread (resolved 2026-09-14):** Emma's late father's real-estate/
  development company bought and developed land near the lake/orchard
  around the time Daniel disappeared. Her father buried evidence to
  protect the business; Emma inherited the company and, going through his
  old files, found what he hid. Protecting the business (and his memory)
  is what she's protecting now.
