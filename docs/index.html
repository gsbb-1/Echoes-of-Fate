Echoes of Fate

Six lives in Willow Creek, a small town in the mountains, circle the same eighteen-year-old disappearance — an artist who keeps painting a place she has never been, a stranger looking for his missing brother, and four others who have each kept some piece of the truth to themselves. A contemporary novel about fate, choice, and how one person's decisions ripple into the lives of everyone around them.
