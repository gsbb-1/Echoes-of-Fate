# Scene plan: Chapter 24 — "Reckoning"

Ensemble climax at the festival. Olivia walks to Jack believing she is
confessing to withholding evidence of murder; the folder and her account
are set against each other — with Colby's ch. 19 admission already in
Jack's head — and the true shape (accident, not murder; cover-up, not
killing) comes clear to everyone at once, Olivia included. The pocket
watch gets its payoff: Michael connects the recovered watch to the glint
in Sarah's painting. Reveal discipline ends here — everything assembles.

- **Beat 1 — the confession.** Olivia meets Jack at the edge of the
  square, the interview-room fear of an 18-year-old on her. She tells
  him, plainly, the whole version she has lived with: she was there that
  night, at the treeline, waiting to meet Daniel; two figures struggling
  in bad light near the pond; voices, then a splash, then silence; a
  truck arriving shortly after and men going down to the water's edge;
  she believed she witnessed the aftermath of Warren Hale killing him,
  and has stayed silent out of fear and shame — and for Liam — ever
  since.
- **Beat 2 — the truth set against the folder.** Jack lets her finish,
  then lays it out: she saw Colby, not a murder — the argument, the
  bank giving way, Colby's panicked call, Hale's machine burying the
  boy and the ground under "erosion control." He reads the folder to her
  (and to the gathered circle): the unfiled inspection, Warren's note
  ordering the regrade finished before questions, the cancelled
  severance check. The relief-and-grief beat: not a murder she watched;
  a different, sadder thing she watched the cover-up of.
- **Beat 3 — everyone at once.** Emma present and unmasked — the folder
  was hers, she carried it to Jack; watching her father's arithmetic land
  in public. Michael and Sarah; Liam at the tent edge; the truth a
  shared room. Michael hears the last piece: from Colby to the folder to
  Olivia, the whole shape.
- **Beat 4 — the watch.** The county finished with the effects; Jack
  brings the pocket watch — inscribed *Daniel, 2004* — and gives it to
  Michael. The glint in Sarah's painting (ch. 15's plant) comes level:
  the pale line in the corner of the canvas is the watch in his hand,
  the last thing his brother touched, and Sarah's painting has carried
  it for eighteen years without knowing. Michael connects it on-page.
- **Beat 5 — the aftermath's edge.** The festival carries on around the
  held circle; each person left alone with the shape-a-moment — Olivia's
  shoulder settling; Emma's inheritance settled; Michael and Sarah at
  the painting. A quiet coda pointing at fallout (ch. 25) without
  resolving it. ~1,900-2,400 words.

## Craft notes
- Ensembles with one acted-on stage: the confession reads in the open,
  the reveal reads from objects (folder, watch, painting) as much as
  speech.
- Watch the exactness of ch. 18's *watch* being the connection point the
  reader already holds. ~2,000-2,300 words.
