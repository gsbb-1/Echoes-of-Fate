# Scene plan: Chapter 2 — "What the Morning Hides"

Ensemble chapter, three scenes (Michael, Emma, Liam), same morning as
Chapter 1. Introduction-tier per `scenes/plot-map.md`: still no names
attached to the mystery. Binding device continues: the same construction
equipment/groundbreaking reaches each of these three too.

## Scene 1 — Michael

- **Goal:** find the site named (vaguely) in Daniel's postcard.
- **Obstacle:** only has vague directions; has to ask around town, which
  he hates doing.
- **Outcome:** doesn't find it yet, but passes a café window with a
  painting in it that snags his attention without his knowing why (Sarah's
  work — first indirect contact between the two threads).

## Scene 2 — Emma

- **Goal:** get through a board call about the stalled parcel without
  committing to anything.
- **Obstacle:** the groundbreaking news is now public; she can't stall
  further today.
- **Outcome:** recites the board-approved line, almost says something
  true, doesn't. Ends looking at where the folder is hidden.

## Scene 3 — Liam

- **Goal:** finish his assigned box of newspapers at the historical
  society archive so he can leave.
- **Obstacle:** gets snagged by an old front page ("Local Man Still
  Missing") — not because he cares about the story, but because of a
  detail in the photo.
- **Outcome:** sets it aside to "look at later" instead of filing it —
  first, small, unknowing act of curiosity. Chapter ends on the same
  distant engine noise from Chapter 1, now audible from the archive
  window.
