# Scene plan: Chapter 1 — "Six Lives, One Town"

Ensemble chapter, three scenes (Sarah, Jack, Olivia), per
`docs/workflows/scene-planning.md`. Introduction-tier per
`scenes/plot-map.md`: establish private discomfort, name nothing about the
mystery yet. Binding device: each scene ends near the same real-world
event — construction equipment moving toward the edge of town for the
Hale groundbreaking — without any character knowing its significance.

## Scene 1 — Sarah

- **Goal:** finish a commissioned piece before the café owner collects
  work for the window display this afternoon.
- **Obstacle:** the lake/orchard image has crept into the corner of the
  commissioned piece without her noticing until now; her mother calls
  mid-work and she doesn't pick up.
- **Outcome:** she scrapes the corner back to bare canvas and starts over,
  behind schedule, unsettled without being able to say why.

## Scene 2 — Jack

- **Goal:** get through his morning coffee at the diner without touching
  the Voss file box today.
- **Obstacle:** overhears diner talk that the long-stalled lakeside parcel
  is finally breaking ground; his hands go cold before his mind catches up.
- **Outcome:** goes home and takes the box down anyway.

## Scene 3 — Olivia

- **Goal:** get through a double shift without the money math running in
  the back of her head the whole time.
- **Obstacle:** a text from Liam that he'll be late (community service at
  the historical society) snags a flicker of unease she can't place and
  doesn't have time for.
- **Outcome:** pushes it down, keeps working. Chapter ends on the distant
  sound of heavy equipment starting up at the edge of town.
