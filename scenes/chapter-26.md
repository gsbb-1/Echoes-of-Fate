# Scene plan: Chapter 26 — "Echoes of Fate"

Ensemble resolution, some weeks later; the festival is over, autumn
deepening into the first true cold. Reflection on fate, choice, and
interconnectedness carried in action, not speech: the loops closing, the
final image. Mirror the openings of Ch. 1-2 (the same town, the same
six, the same morning light) — now with the weights gone.

- **Beat 1 — the town's morning.** The same light as Ch. 1: low and gold
  through east windows. The town square, ordinary, no storm. Each life
  at its settled morning, quietly — six small touches that rhyme with
  their first appearances (Sarah at her paints, Jack at the diner, Olivia
  at the counter, Liam at the archive, Emma at the glass, Michael in the
  street) without repeating them.
- **Beat 2 — Sarah paints forward.** In her studio: a new canvas, not
  compelled — a facing-of-the-ground-with-the-knowledge piece, or
  something wholly her own. The compel is gone; the work is chosen. The
  small watch-glint, now known, finds its deliberate place in the corner:
  painted on purpose for the first time.
- **Beat 3 — loose ends closed.** Quiet closures, near-addresses rather
  than scenes: the parcel marked memorial ground; Jack's file sealed and
  the diner stool just a stool; Liam at the archive finishing the last
  boxes, Mrs. Okafor's blessing now fulfilled; Olivia's drawer opened and
  the bracelet taken out and looked at — or left, safely, where it is.
- **Beat 4 — Michael stays.** Michael with Sarah at the lakefront
  memorial ground — the place Sarah painted, the place Danny died — no
  longer a scene to be solved. He decides, without a scene about
  deciding, to stay: the motel room given up, a place of his own. The
  pocket watch with him; the broken one, at last, repaired or put by.
  Time moves forward from 4:47.
- **Beat 5 — final image.** Evening; the six lives crossing the same
  square the way they did in Ch. 1/Ch. 2, each carrying less; fate and
  choice braided in one image — the painting, the memorial ground, the
  town at rest under its old story finally told true. Final frame: the
  deliberate glint in the corner of the new canvas, painted on purpose,
  catching the last of the light.

## Craft notes
- Deliberately quieter register; the book ends on forgiveness and
  forward motion, not a twist.
- Mirror the intro chapters' imagery so the reader feels the loop close
  structurally.
- The watch motif resolves: involuntary → deliberate; stopped → moving.
  ~1,900-2,200 words.
