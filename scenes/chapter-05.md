# Scene plan: Chapter 5 — "The Ledger"

Single scene, Emma POV. Night before the Thursday groundbreaking set up in
Chapter 2. Emma has already known about the folder for ~18 months
(`scenes/plot-map.md`: sealed folder, found while settling the estate) —
this chapter is the night she finally reconciles its contents against the
company books. Character-Development tier per `scenes/plot-map.md`: Emma
and the reader learn what the folder *means*, nothing surfaces to anyone
else, and Olivia/Michael/Jack threads stay off-page.

- **Goal:** one last pass through her father's old files before the ground
  moves — to make the folder either explainable (so she can stop carrying
  it) or actionable (so she must act tonight). She wants a version where
  the ledger stays clean.
- **Obstacle:** the math won't clear. Each of the three items (the unfiled
  safety inspection, Warren's note to Colby, the cancelled severance check)
  has an innocent single explanation; together they couple into one ugly
  pattern. Tonight she finds a layer she'd missed on earlier passes: the
  check's ledger entry was predated out of its real week into the previous
  quarter — the correction in her father's own hand.
- **Turn:** she recognizes the classicism of the correction — the exact
  care that taught her to keep a ledger clean is the instrument of the
  burying. She can neither discharge the folder nor destroy it without
  becoming someone she isn't; she locks it away again and lets tomorrow
  happen, and the chapter buttons on that being the decision she's been
  making for eighteen months.
- **Craft note:** Emma's established voice (`characters/emma.md`) — complete
  controlled sentences, exact figures served cold, ledger/land/clean-title
  metaphor; her thumbnail habit and her father's watch as physical anchors.
  Single interior scene, but keep it object-anchored (folder, check,
  ledger, handwriting) so it reads as action, not navel-gazing.
