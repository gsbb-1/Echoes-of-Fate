# Scene plan: Chapter 23 — "The Gathering Storm"

Ensemble chapter, festival-run-up. The emotional center is Emma: the
post has run ahead of her, the county's file is open, and the folder
sitting in her study can no longer stay where it is without being found
independently — so the choice she has avoided since ch. 6 lands, and she
moves it herself, handing it to Jack the evening before the festival.
Against that, the town gathers; every strand is present in the same
square. Reveal discipline: the folder's contents surface to Jack alone
(fulfilling plot-map's "she brings it to him rather than have it found"),
never in public; the truth stays unassembled in the room.

- **Beat 1 — the post's reach (Michael/Sarah).** The society's page
  number climbs; Michael, at a quiet end of the festival setup, watches
  his brother's face carry through the town's chatter. Sarah is with him
  (they have squared her ground now); the festival and the exhibit pull
  the whole valley in. Michael's watch stays at 4:47.
- **Beat 2 — Emma's decision.** Inside Hale Development, then Emma's
  study at night: the folder out, once, examined — the inspection, her
  father's note to Colby ("finish the regrade before anyone comes back
  with more questions"), the cancelled severance check. The county has
  asked for the company's records on the parcel; discovery is a matter of
  time. She carries it to Jack's door and hands it over the way you set
  down a decision — the turn of her whole arc. He reads it as she
  watches; the parallel to Colby's account in ch. 19 closes in, on Jack's
  side of the room only. She names her stall, her 18 months, her
  paralysis; Jack holds the written half of the cover-up.
- **Beat 3 — festival morning, all six present.** The square fills:
  stalls, the society's exhibit tent with the archive accession and the
  two-photo post printed large enough for a crowd; Sarah's painting hung
  in the loan corner; Liam working the archive table; Olivia, festival
  week hours, at the edge; Emma present with her company's booth; Michael
  and Jack passing the same ground. Every thread in one place for the
  first time.
- **Beat 4 — the storm breaks toward Olivia.** The county investigator
  at the tent, drawing Liam aside about the post (whose account, whose
  mother, what she knew) in view of the crowd; an old Hale crew man
  muttering about the girl in the photo dragging a good name through it.
  Olivia watches the cost land on her boy — the thing Ch. 22's close
  promised ("she will not let it come for her boy"). The festival sounds
  resume; Olivia's decision crystallizes across the square toward where
  Jack stands.

## Craft notes
- Ensemble, sectioned; Emma's section is the longest single block — the
  emotional spine of the chapter is her choice, not exposition.
- The folder contents are named for the reader but spoken only to Jack,
  in private, keeping Ch. 24's public reveal intact.
- The wash of festival texture does the "all six present" work without
  six POV sections. ~1,900-2,200 words.
