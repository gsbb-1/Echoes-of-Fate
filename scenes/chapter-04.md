# Scene plan: Chapter 4 — "A Name Across the Room"

Single scene, Michael POV. First dialogue-heavy chapter — apply
`research/dialogue-craft.md` directly.

- **Goal:** find out who painted the piece with the water image (a lead,
  however thin, on the place he's looking for) without revealing why he's
  asking.
- **Obstacle:** the only way to find out is to talk to people, which he's
  bad at and avoids; when he finally asks at the café, he's pointed at
  Sarah directly, in person, with no time to prepare an approach.
- **Scene:** Michael approaches Sarah under a thin pretext (interested in
  buying/commissioning a piece). Sarah, unnerved by recognizing him as the
  starer from across the street, is guarded but also professionally
  curious about a stranger who wants to talk about her work specifically.
  Both want something from the exchange beyond politeness and don't get
  it: he's fishing for where the water image comes from; she's sizing him
  up (subject to paint, or threat to her routine) and testing whether
  he'll actually answer a direct question. Neither fully succeeds.
- **Turn:** she asks, half-joking, half not, why he's really asking. He
  deflects. The conversation ends unresolved but both have decided,
  privately, that they want another one.
