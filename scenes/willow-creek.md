# Willow Creek

The story's setting: a small town in the mountains.

## Established (from Chapter 1 draft)

- Nestled in a mountain valley
- Streets lined with quaint shops and cozy cafes
- "A place where time seemed to slow down"
- Air is fresh, people know each other by name
- Has at least: studio apartments, a business district (Emma's office), a
  park with a bench, residential apartments on the outskirts (Olivia's)
- Picturesque surface, but residents "harbored secrets and burdens"
