# Scene plan: Chapter 3 — "The Artist and the Stranger"

Single scene, Sarah POV.

- **Goal:** ask Owen whether he'd give her a real show — her own work, not
  the tourist commissions — using the piece he just collected as her case.
- **Obstacle:** nerve. Every rehearsed version of the ask collapses the
  moment she's actually standing in front of him; her mother's voice
  ("do something sensible") keeps surfacing at the worst moments.
- **Turn:** while she's outside working up to going in, she clocks a man
  across the street who was, unmistakably, staring at her painting in the
  café window the other day — and is doing something like it again, at a
  distance, this time at her. Neither approaches. She goes in, asks Owen
  half the question she meant to ask, and leaves without resolving either
  thread — the show, or the man.
- **Craft note:** first appearance of Michael from another POV — per
  `research/multi-pov-structure.md`'s "let POV characters appear in each
  other's chapters before their own POV chapter together."
