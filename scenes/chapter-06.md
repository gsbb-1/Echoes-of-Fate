# Scene plan: Chapter 6 — "What Emma Won't Say"

Single scene with a short button, Emma POV. The groundbreaking ceremony at
the lakeside parcel (Chapter 2's "we break ground Thursday"), with a press
photographer, the board, and Priya. Character-Development tier per
`scenes/plot-map.md` — no remains, no other-thread reveals; the reader only
sees Emma nearly let the truth surface and pull it back.

- **Goal:** get through the ceremony — remarks, shovel photo, the board
  watching — without a leak, projecting the company's and her father's
  clean legacy onto a ground that is anything but.
- **Obstacle:** she stands on the exact ground her father ordered regraded
  to bury a death, and Priya's diligence nearly surfaces it aloud: pulling
  the site's payroll history for the press kit, Priya notices a foreman
  (Hank Colby) whose records simply stop in autumn 2008 — no exit
  paperwork, no final timesheet. Emma deflects with a precisely-true
  answer, and the truthful sentence ("he didn't leave") almost comes out
  anyway before the ceremony interrupts her.
- **Turn:** in her remarks she reaches a sentence that is a half-step from
  true ("this ground has waited") and pulls it back in view of everyone.
  Afterward, alone by the old backfilled section next to the pond, she
  recognizes that the person she has been protecting all along — her
  father's memory, the clean ledger — is exactly the thing the cover-up
  was for, and that she's been the responsible custodian of the same
  decision. She buries the recognition again, but the reader has seen it.
- **Craft note:** Emma's controlled voice against a public event —
  schedule-exact observations, status markers on the attendees, minimal
  free-indirect confession; undercharge the interiority the way she would.
  Dialogue carries the near-tell (apply `research/dialogue-craft.md`).
