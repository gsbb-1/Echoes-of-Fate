# Scene plan: Chapter 9 — "Two Jobs, One Boy"

Single through-line day with a home button, Olivia POV. Character-Development
tier per `scenes/plot-map.md` — no new facts surface; the reader gets
Olivia's side of the Ch. 7 diner moment (she saw Jack with a stranger) as
dramatic irony, plus the first real friction: Liam's innocent archive
enthusiasm about the missing man landing on his mother's eighteen-year-old
wound.

- **Goal:** get through the day — diner shift, night cleaning at Hale
  Development — and get home with enough of herself left to be Liam's
  mother.
- **Obstacle:** the two jobs are the shape of her life ("Two Jobs, One
  Boy"), and on top of the usual grind comes the old fear rekindled by Jack
  Muldoon having sat his diner booth with a man she didn't know — plus
  Liam, home late from the archive, cheerfully talking about the man she
  still can't name even to herself.
- **Turn:** Liam shows her the search-line photograph on his phone and
  asks, lightly, "you'd remember that — you were here," and she absorbs it
  in the way she absorbs everything, and the chapter ends with the two of
  them on opposite sides of a wall: his music through the door, her holding
  the fragment she will not name — dark, raised voices, a splash, truck
  lights — and doing the one thing she knows how to do with it, which is
  nothing.
- **Craft note:** Olivia's economical, triaged voice (time/cost before
  anything else; shorter, clipped sentences under strain). Memory fragment
  stays strictly fragmentary per `research/mystery-plotting.md` — circle
  the fact, never name it. Include the Hale-office night-cleaning beat
  (locked drawer in the corner office) as an irony plant for Ch. 22, with
  Olivia touching nothing locked.
