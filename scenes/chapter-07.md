# Scene plan: Chapter 7 — "Cold Case"

Single scene, Jack POV. The diner, a mid-morning — Jack's routine, the last
place he expects a piece of the file he never had. Character-Development
tier per `scenes/plot-map.md` (the informal reopening); the reader learns
the postcard's full text — including a lead line about the orchard ground
north of the lake that Ch. 2 only alluded to when it quoted the card.

- **Goal:** get through an ordinary coffee and not get pulled back into a
  case he's spent six years learning to leave in the box.
- **Obstacle:** Michael — sitting down at his stool with eighteen years'
  worth of accusation in a level voice, knowing which buttons to press, and
  then producing the one piece of the file Jack never had. Jack's caginess
  with relatives running their own investigations (`characters/jack.md`)
  fights the pull of genuinely new evidence.
- **Turn:** reading the card — "found something out here Hale's not going
  to like" landing a name, Hale, on a parcel Jack's own file already maps —
  and realizing he's agreed to take it before his mouth said so. Michael
  leaves the postcard with him. Jack walks home and takes the file box
  down, further than he's allowed himself to in years.
- **Craft note:** Jack's procedural, catalogue-rhythm voice; what's out of
  place first (a man who orders nothing). Olivia in the background of the
  diner — Jack clocking her stillness at the stranger and stopping himself
  from reading anything into it: a behavioral plant, kept at Jack's-suspicion
  tier, not shown as fact. Keep the postcard text consistent with Ch. 2.
