# Scene plan: Chapter 21 — "Alliances"

Michael POV. The post from Ch. 20 reaches Michael within a day, and the
characters align: Jack, Sarah, and Michael knit together as the Harvest
Festival approaches, Michael reads a tell off Emma face to face, and he
finally stands in front of the woman in his brother's photograph. Reveal
discipline: no folder contents, no Olivia confession (that's Ch. 24), Emma
denies only, the shape stays unproven.

- **Beat 1 — the post.** Michael, watching the society's exhibit page
  (he's followed it since Sarah flagged the exhibit), sees Liam's post —
  the caption, the summer field, the search line — and his brother at the
  edge of a stranger's kept photograph, unmistakable. The click: Danny had
  a girl in this town nobody knew; and the boy who posted it is hers, and
  the photo has a felt-tip date, the summer before. Michael pulls Jack in.
- **Beat 2 — Jack names the girl.** Jack recognizes the teenager in the
  summer photo as Olivia Marsh, the one witness he could never get to
  move; the boy who posted is her son, Liam — whom Jack has already met at
  the archive ("You're Olivia's boy"). The old thread runs fresh: Olivia
  knew Danny — a secret, early, unofficial. Michael: "Then she's the piece
  I go to."
- **Beat 3 — the Emma meeting.** The visit Ch. 19 set up: Michael at Hale
  Development, named as Daniel Voss's brother (he read her tell months
  ago at the office as a stranger; now he arrives as himself). Emma, exact,
  denies — the company has no record, pending identification, nothing to
  give him. Michael holds her with the catalogue-man calm and names what
  he's decided: his brother is under ground her father's company put him
  into, and whatever it takes, he'll stand there until the county looks.
  She almost breaks, and doesn't. He leaves knowing the tell is real.
- **Beat 4 — the Olivia approach.** Michael at the diner, to the woman who
  wiped the counter while the two men talked in the booth weeks ago. He
  says who he is; the girl in her son's photograph is his brother. She
  deflects, flat, practiced, without acknowledging the girl is her.
  Michael: he's not accusing her — she's the last person alive who knew
  Danny, and the county will ask her, and he'd rather she looked at his
  brother than at a badge. He leaves the truth on her table. The chapter
  ends with the alliances drawn: Jack, Sarah, Michael — and two women who
  hold two ends of the same silence both knowing he's coming.

## Craft notes
- Michael's exact catalogue-man voice; his reads are conclusions, dry.
  Two confrontations (Emma, Olivia) that rhyme — scripted paper vs. flat
  deflection — with his tell-reading the through-line.
- The diner beat must honor Ch. 9: Olivia already saw "the stranger in
  the booth with Jack"; she just never knew who he was. Michael's arrival
  completes her dread.
- No confession, no folder, no watch payoff (later). ~1,900-2,200
  words.
