# Scene plan: Chapter 18 — "The Weight of It"

Emma & Jack. The midpoint escalation (per `scenes/plot-map.md`): grading
equipment reaches the old bank and turns up remains; Emma is confronted by
consequence, Jack by an official reopening. Tier-3 confirmation (Daniel is
dead) without how/by whom. Reveal discipline: Emma does NOT yet bring the
folder to Jack (that only happens in the run-up to the climax, forced by
Liam's Ch. 20 post); Olivia does not speak (Ch. 16 planted her response —
the discovery alone isn't enough); the Colby confrontation stays ~Ch. 19;
the identity stays "pending dental" so the name is never strictly leapt to.

- **Beat 1 — Emma, the call.** Her office; the foreman's phone call — the
  dozer's blade hit something in the north bank, "you need to come out."
  The drive out; the stalled machines; the tape; the sheriff; the halted
  ground she knows the contents of. The public line (old ground, a
  landslide, an accident), and the private weight — she's had the folder
  for eighteen months. She speaks to no one about it. Home that night:
  faces the house, the safe, the drawer — and does not move the folder
  yet, though she knows she will be forced to. The phone ringing unanswered
  (press). Her resolve from Ch. 14 ("move it myself before it's found")
  meets the event and stalls — the paralysis is the consequence.
- **Beat 2 — Jack, the news.** He hears it the way old cases reach you —
  the deputy, a favor owed, or Ruth's coffee stop carrying the town's
  one-sentence version. Jack at the tape line reads the ground the way he's
  read it for eighteen years: the bank he flagged, the regrade he never
  got a work order for, the body that was always somewhere in the math.
  The county takes it over — the case leaves his hands and becomes
  official. Evidence note: a personal effect among the recovered items, a
  watch with an inscription, being run — let the reader register the Ch.
  12/15 glint's object without spending Michael's payoff (he connects it
  to the painting later, on-page).
- **Beat 3 — going to tell Michael.** Rather than let the town do it, Jack
  walks to the motel to give the brother the formal shape of it first: the
  ground kept its secret, and it gave it up under a dozer. Ends on the walk
  up the motel stairs — the weight, the reopening, the case Jack never
  closed finally closed by machinery.

## Craft notes
- Two POVs in one chapter (Emma section, `---`, Jack section), each ~half.
  Emma: exact, bureaucratic over-control under pressure; her interior runs
  on the ledger/red-line language. Jack: what's out of place as compass;
  dry; the private-file man vs the public case.
- Do NOT have anyone assert the remains are Daniel's outright — "pending
  dental" keeps the official uncertainty (and the reader's knowledge can
  outrun the town's). Keeps the discipline clean for Ch. 19-24.
