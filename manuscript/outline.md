# Echoes of Fate — Outline

**Type of literature:** Contemporary fiction blending mystery, drama, and
romance. Six interconnected character arcs explore fate, choice, and how one
person's decisions ripple into the lives of others.

**Setting:** Willow Creek, a small town in the mountains. See
[scenes/willow-creek.md](../scenes/willow-creek.md).

**Timeframe:** roughly six weeks in early autumn, culminating at the town's
annual Harvest Festival.

**Target length:** ~200 pages, 26 chapters, ~1,900-2,200 words per chapter
(~50,000-57,000 words total). This supersedes the original 13-chapter/
~50-page target (see `archive/chatgpt-sessions/` for that earlier version).

## Characters

See [characters/](../characters/) for full per-character profiles. Summary:

- **Sarah** — a young artist trying to break free from her past
- **Michael** — searching Willow Creek for the truth about his brother
  Daniel, who vanished eighteen years ago
- **Emma** — a businesswoman hiding evidence her late father buried about
  Daniel's disappearance
- **Jack** — the retired detective who never solved Daniel's case
- **Olivia** — a single mother who witnessed something the night Daniel
  vanished and has never told anyone
- **Liam** — Olivia's rebellious teenage son, who unknowingly surfaces the
  buried connection

## The central mystery

What happened to Daniel eighteen years ago is the connective mechanism
tying all six threads together — see `characters/README.md` for the
decided facts and `scenes/plot-map.md` (once written) for the detailed
beat-by-beat reveal design.

## Story structure (6-part thematic shape)

The 26 chapters are distributed across the original 6-part shape:

| Part | Chapters | Pages (approx) | Shape |
|------|----------|-----------------|-------|
| I. Introduction | 1-4 | ~30 | Introduce the six characters and Willow Creek |
| II. Character Development | 5-10 | ~46 | Each character's personal struggle and inciting incident |
| III. Rising Action | 11-16 | ~46 | Threads start crossing; investigation and secrets advance |
| IV. Midpoint Revelations | 17-20 | ~31 | First real reveals; consequences land |
| V. Climax | 21-24 | ~31 | Convergence at the Harvest Festival; confrontation |
| VI. Resolution | 25-26 | ~15 | Fallout, reflection, close |
