# Echoes of Fate — Outline

**Type of literature:** Contemporary fiction blending mystery, drama, and
romance. Six interconnected character arcs explore fate, choice, and how one
person's decisions ripple into the lives of others.

**Setting:** Willow Creek, a small town nestled in the mountains.

**Target length:** ~50 pages, 13 chapters.

## Characters

See [characters/](../characters/) for full per-character profiles. Summary:

- **Sarah** — a young artist trying to break free from her past
- **Michael** — a mysterious stranger with a troubled past
- **Emma** — a successful businesswoman hiding a secret
- **Jack** — a retired detective haunted by an unsolved case
- **Olivia** — a struggling single mother trying to make ends meet
- **Liam** — a rebellious teenager searching for his place in the world

## Story structure

1. **Introduction** — introduce the six characters and Willow Creek; establish
   the theme of interconnected fate and choice.
2. **Character development** — each character's personal struggle is
   established (see character bible).
3. **Plot** — Sarah meets Michael and is drawn into a web of intrigue; Emma's
   secret threatens to surface; Jack's past resurfaces through a new case;
   Olivia is forced into a difficult decision; Liam's rebellion has
   unforeseen consequences; all six threads begin to intertwine.
4. **Climax** — the six characters converge to confront their destinies;
   secrets are revealed, relationships tested, lives changed.
5. **Resolution** — each character reckons with their choices and the role
   fate played; the connections between their stories are revealed in a
   twist.
6. **Conclusion** — reflection on fate, choice, and interconnectedness.
